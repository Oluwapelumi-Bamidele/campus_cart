# CampusCart POS pro  business App

> **A simple terminal tool for campus sellers to track stock, calculate totals, and print receipts.**

---

##  Startup Vision

Many student business owners and campus vendors do not have expensive store systems or reliable internet. 

**CampusCart** solves this problem by providing a fast command-line tool that works offline. It helps sellers track stock, calculate customer bills, and print simple receipts with zero setup cost.

### Who Is It For?
* **Student Businesses:** Pop-up shops, bake sales, and clothing sellers.
* **Campus Vendors:** Small food stalls and kiosks.
* **Student Clubs:** Ticket sales and event fundraising.

### Why Use It?
* **Free to Run:** Works directly inside your computer terminal.
* **Fast Checkout:** Quick keyboard inputs save time during busy hours.
* **Works Offline:** Saves your sales and stock data directly on your computer.
